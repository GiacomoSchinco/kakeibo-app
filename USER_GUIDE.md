# Kakeibo App - User Guide

## What is Kakeibo?

Kakeibo (家計簿) is a Japanese budgeting method that focuses on mindful spending. This app helps you track your income and expenses while following this traditional approach.

## Getting Started

### 1. Create an Account
- Visit the app and click "Sign Up"
- Enter your email and create a password (minimum 6 characters)
- Confirm your password and submit

### 2. Sign In
- Use your email and password to sign in
- You'll be redirected to the dashboard

## Features

### Dashboard
The dashboard provides an overview of your current month's finances:
- **Summary Cards**: View total income, expenses, and balance
- **Income vs Expenses Chart**: Bar chart comparing income and expenses
- **Category Breakdown**: Pie chart showing expense distribution by category
- **Recent Transactions**: Quick view of your latest 5 transactions

### Transactions Page
Manage all your financial records:
- **Add Transaction**: Click the "Add Transaction" button to record income or expenses
- **Filter**: Use filters to view specific date ranges, categories, or transaction types
- **Edit**: Click "Edit" on any transaction to modify it
- **Delete**: Click "Delete" to remove a transaction (with confirmation)

### Adding a Transaction
1. Click "Add Transaction"
2. Choose type: Income or Expense
3. Enter the amount
4. Select a category
5. Pick the date
6. (Optional) Add a description
7. Click "Add"

## Tips for Using Kakeibo Method

1. **Track Everything**: Record all income and expenses, no matter how small
2. **Review Regularly**: Check your dashboard weekly to stay aware of your spending
3. **Use Categories Wisely**: Categorize expenses accurately for better insights
4. **Set Goals**: Use the balance information to set savings targets
5. **Be Mindful**: Before each purchase, ask yourself if it's a need or a want

## Data Privacy

- All connections are encrypted
- You can delete your data at any time
